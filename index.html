Reaction Time Test

Five trials. Tap as fast as you can when the cue fires.

Cue mode

  • Visual: the screen turns green — tap the moment it does.
  • Audio: the screen stays dark — tap when you hear the whistle.
  • Both: green flash and beep together.
  • Random mix: each trial is independently visual, audio, or both.
  • Tap before the cue and the trial restarts as a false start.
